# 🌾 Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

📊 **Data Visualization & Statistical Analysis Project**

## 📌 About the Project

Agricultural performance varies across seasons due to changes in environmental conditions, farming practices, resource availability, and economic factors.

This project analyzes agricultural data across **Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid seasons** to identify seasonal patterns, compare agricultural performance, investigate relationships between conditions and outcomes, and generate meaningful insights.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Explore and understand the agricultural dataset
- Clean and prepare the data for analysis
- Compare agricultural performance across seasons
- Analyze environmental and farming conditions
- Study resource usage and water efficiency
- Compare crop-wise and regional performance
- Analyze economic performance and profitability
- Identify significant patterns and unusual observations
- Apply statistical techniques and data visualizations
- Generate evidence-based insights and recommendations

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains **4,000 records and 28 variables** covering:

🌦️ Environmental conditions  
🌱 Crop and farming information  
📍 Geographic information  
💧 Resource usage  
🌾 Yield and production  
📊 Water efficiency  
💰 Market price, cost, revenue, and profit  
📅 Seasonal information

## 🌦️ Seasonal Analysis

The analysis focuses on:

**🌧️ Kharif | ❄️ Rabi | ☀️ Zaid**

Some key observations include:

- Kharif has the highest median yield among the three seasons.
- Kharif has a higher proportion of profitable farms.
- Rainfall and humidity are comparatively higher during Kharif.
- Zaid experiences comparatively higher temperatures.
- Water efficiency varies across seasons.
- Statistical testing shows significant seasonal differences in selected measures such as yield and profit.

> Note: Correlation and statistical association do not necessarily imply causation.

## 🧹 Data Quality

The dataset contains **120 missing cells** across selected variables.

Missing values were investigated rather than blindly removing rows, and unnecessary imputation was avoided. Analyses involving a particular variable use the available valid observations.

Outliers were investigated and retained in the dataset rather than being automatically removed.
